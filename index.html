03 The Journey

Four Decades
In The Making.

From one visionary's consultancy in 1985 to a multi-company group serving India's heaviest industries. Every milestone below was earned on the shop floor.

1985

The Vision

After decades in heavy chemicals and engineering across India and abroad, founder Shri. Ramesh Manglik sets out on his own: chemical and timber trading, plus consultancy for heavy chemical plants.

1987

Rasyantrik Udyog

Seeing how poor-quality equipment was holding chemical industries back, he establishes an engineering unit in Chandrapur to manufacture chemical plant machinery.

1995

Deepak Engineering

Deepak Manglik, mechanical engineer and MBA, joins and anticipates immense growth in the power sector. A second unit is born to diversify the group.

Visit Deepak Engineering →
2000

Major Expansion

New specialised machines are installed, accompanied by stainless steel equipment and spares fabrication.

2005

Reconditioning Era

Reconditioning and superconditioning of critical power plant components begins: coal burner assemblies, journal assemblies, screw conveyors and more.

2010

Into Cement

Wear-component expertise opens the door to the cement sector with quality spares.

2015

Ferrous Engineering

Demand outgrows capacity. The third unit rises over 1,60,000 sq. ft. at Tadali MIDC, specializing in heavy engineering jobs up to 15 tons.

Visit Ferrous Engineering →
2017

Defence Breakthrough

First Ordnance Factory order: Pinaka rocket pods, followed by several more defence contracts.

2018

EWAC Authorised

Becomes the EWAC authorised reclamation centre in Central India.

2019

Hardox® Certified

Authorised Hardox® Wearparts Center, joining SSAB's global network of fewer than 600 certified partners.

2020

Ferrous Automations

Launch of Ferrous Automations Pvt. Ltd. in Chakan, Pune for automated material handling equipment.

Visit Ferrous Automations →
NOW

Trusted Pan India

A reputation built on in-house manufacturing, reliable systems and real-world experience earned with prestigious clients across power, steel, cement, automation and defence.
