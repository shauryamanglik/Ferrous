Sales & Marketing Engineer

01About The Role

We are looking for an energetic, technically sound Sales & Marketing Engineer to drive our growth. Based at Tadali MIDC but on the road often, you will be the face of the company to clients in power, cement, steel and defence. This is a field role: plant visits, shop-floor conversations, and closing heavy engineering contracts, backed by the digital and offline marketing you drive yourself.

02What You’ll Do
01

Field Sales & Client Acquisition

Travel to industrial sites, plants and corporate offices to pitch our fabrication, machining and reconditioning capability.

02

Client Relationships

Own existing accounts. Visit regularly, find the operational pain, and propose the fix using Hardox® wear parts or EWAC reclamation.

03

Technical Presentations

Present to plant managers, maintenance heads and procurement teams, technically and commercially.

04

Marketing Execution

Run offline and digital marketing: brochures, industrial expos and a professional digital presence that pulls inbound B2B leads.

05

Market Intelligence

Track upcoming projects, plant shutdowns and competitor moves to position us early for vendor registrations and orders.

06

Sales Coordination

Work with estimating, engineering and commercial so client requirements turn into proposals that actually win.

03What We’re Looking For

Must Have 5

  • B.E. / B.Tech or Diploma in Mechanical Engineering or similar
  • Minimum 2 years in B2B industrial sales, technical marketing or business development
  • Exceptional interpersonal, negotiation and communication skills
  • Willing to travel frequently across Central India and pan-India
  • Able to read technical drawings and understand heavy machinery components

Nice To Have 3

  • MBA or a qualification in Sales or Marketing
  • Experience in heavy engineering, machinery or manufacturing
  • Fluent in English, Hindi and Marathi
04Perks & Benefits

Revenue Ownership

A high-impact role driving the top line of a 40-year-old legacy company.

Incentives & Travel

Travel allowances and performance-based incentives on top of a competitive base.

Room Access

Direct exposure to top management at India's largest industrial conglomerates.

Technical Backup

A strong engineering team behind every promise you make on the field.

Free On-Site Housing

Accommodation for bachelors and families can be provided on request, subject to availability.
